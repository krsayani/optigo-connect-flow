# OptiGo: Connecting Optical

Build a complete, polished, investor-ready website for a healthcare technology startup called:

OPTIGO

ABOUT THE COMPANY

OptiGo is a modern software infrastructure company being built specifically for the optometry and optical industry.

Our initial product connects optometry practices, practice-management/EHR systems, optical laboratories, and patients into one streamlined workflow.

Today, optical ordering is fragmented. Practice staff often work across EHRs, lab portals, phone calls, emails, manual data entry, and disconnected systems just to get a pair of glasses ordered, track its progress, and communicate updates to the patient.

OptiGo is being built to simplify and automate that process.

Our goal is for optical order information to move seamlessly from the practice/EHR into OptiGo, through the appropriate laboratory workflow, and back to the practice and patient as the order progresses.

OptiGo should become the connectivity and automation layer between:

PRACTICE / EHR

↓

OPTIGO

↓

OPTICAL LAB

↓

REAL-TIME STATUS

↓

PRACTICE + PATIENT

OptiGo is starting with optical workflow automation, but the company has a significantly larger long-term vision for the optometry industry.
